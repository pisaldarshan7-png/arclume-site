PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Arclume handles information when you use the bot and related services.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Overview

Arclume is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, automation, protection, utility, analytics, and community management features. We aim to process only the information reasonably necessary to provide these services.

2. Information Arclume May Process

Depending on the features you use, Arclume may process information such as:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Channel IDs and role IDs.
  • Command names and command interactions.
  • Moderation-related information, such as warnings or actions created through Arclume.
  • Server configuration and feature settings.
  • Technical information needed to operate and protect the service.

3. Message Content

Arclume does not intentionally collect private conversations for advertising or unrelated purposes. Certain moderation, anti-spam, automation, or command features may need to temporarily process message content when required for that feature to work.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide Arclume commands and features.
  • Save server configuration.
  • Perform moderation and automation actions.
  • Detect spam, abuse, or misuse.
  • Maintain security and reliability.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve Arclume's functionality.

5. Data Sharing

Arclume does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to host, operate, secure, or maintain Arclume. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

6. Data Retention

Arclume aims to keep stored information only for as long as it is needed for the feature for which it was collected, security purposes, or legitimate service operation. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of feature or server configuration.

7. Server Administrators

Server administrators decide whether to add Arclume to their Discord server and which permissions and features to enable. Administrators are responsible for configuring Arclume appropriately for their community.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to help protect information processed by Arclume. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Services

Arclume operates through Discord and may rely on third-party infrastructure or hosting providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Removing Arclume

Server administrators can remove Arclume from their Discord server at any time. Some information may remain temporarily where necessary for security, backups, moderation records, or technical operation.

11. Data Requests

Requests concerning information associated with Arclume may be submitted through Arclume's official support channels. We may need reasonable information to verify that a request relates to the correct Discord account or server.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Arclume develops or its features change. The latest version and revision date will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Arclume through the official support channels listed on the Arclume website.
