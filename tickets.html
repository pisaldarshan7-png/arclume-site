05 / STAFF WORKFLOW

Ticket operations

Ticket Relay V2 automatically provides these controls inside active tickets.

👤 Claim / Unclaim

Staff can take ownership of a request and release it again when necessary.

🚨 Priority

Staff can mark tickets Low, Normal, High or Urgent.

➕ Member controls

Add or remove extra members from private tickets.

✏️ Rename

Staff can rename channels without losing the ticket number.

🔒 Close reason

Closing a ticket requests a reason instead of instantly deleting it.

♻️ Reopen

Closed tickets can be reopened by the support team.

📄 Transcript

Arclume generates a text transcript when a ticket is closed.

🗑️ Delete

Staff can permanently delete a ticket after it has been closed.
